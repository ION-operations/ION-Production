# T6 Academic Expansion Plan

**Purpose:** Expand all 9 core systems to T6 academic depth  
**Current:** All T6 documents are skeletons (< 3%)  
**Target:** Academic-level comprehensive documentation

---

## 🎯 **T6 ACADEMIC STRUCTURE (8 Parts)**

Based on CMC and CAS T6 skeletons:

**PART I: Theoretical Foundations** (10,000-12,000 words)
- Introduction and motivation
- Formal model definitions  
- Core theoretical frameworks
- Mathematical foundations
- Theorems with complete proofs

**PART II: Research Literature** (8,000-10,000 words)
- Comprehensive literature review
- Historical evolution of field
- Related work synthesis
- Research gaps identified

**PART III: Mathematical Models** (8,000-10,000 words)
- Complete formal models
- Mathematical rigor throughout
- Complexity analysis
- Information-theoretic bounds

**PART IV: Proofs and Theorems** (6,000-8,000 words)
- Complete proofs for all theorems
- Correctness proofs
- Security property proofs
- Performance bound proofs

**PART V: Historical Context** (4,000-5,000 words)
- Evolution of relevant fields
- Development history
- Key milestones
- Contributions to field

**PART VI: Experimental Results** (6,000-8,000 words)
- Experimental methodology
- Performance benchmarks
- Scalability experiments
- Comparison with alternatives

**PART VII: Comparative Analysis** (4,000-5,000 words)
- Comparison with related systems
- Trade-off analysis
- Suitability assessment

**PART VIII: Future Research** (4,000-5,000 words)
- Open problems
- Research directions
- Collaboration opportunities

**REFERENCES:** 100+ academic citations  
**APPENDICES:** Formal specs, proof details, experimental data
