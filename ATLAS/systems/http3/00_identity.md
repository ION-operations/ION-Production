# HTTP/3 and QUIC — Identity

**Kind:** **IETF** **HTTP/3** **(RFC** **9114)** **over** **QUIC** **(RFC** **9000)** — **UDP-based** **multiplexed** **transport** **with** **TLS** **1.3** **integration** (`DOCUMENTED`, RFCs).

## Boundaries

- **Not** **HTTP/1.1** **or** **HTTP/2** **over** **TCP** — **different** **loss** **recovery** **and** **handshake** **model.**  
- **Not** **gRPC** **—** **though** **deployments** **may** **coexist** **at** **edge** **load** **balancers.**
