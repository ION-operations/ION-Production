## Best-At Answer

- Root host-control surfaces are best at root lane routing.
- Startup or doctrine surfaces are best at durable operating law.
- Correction-packet surfaces are best at targeted corrective override.
- Active work-package law surfaces are best at current-turn authorization.
- Artifact-local boundary surfaces are best at local semantic constraint.

## Local Constraint

No single authority-and-precedence family stands alone as total governing truth on this host:

- root control routes the lane but does not fully authorize the turn,
- doctrine governs operation but does not settle every correction,
- correction packets fix specific failures but do not become a total canon,
- active work packages authorize the current pass but do not replace root or doctrine,
- and artifact boundaries constrain meaning locally but not globally.
