## 📖 Glossary of Key Terms

### **A**

**APOE (Atomic Provenance Orchestration Engine)**
- AIM-OS system for orchestrating multi-agent plans
- Transforms with PLIx: From plan execution to intent achievement
- Reference: Chapter 11

**AST (Abstract Syntax Tree)**
- Tree representation of CNL syntax
- Generated by CNL parser
- Reference: Chapter 13

**Alloy**
- Formal specification language
- Used for PLIx contract validation
- Reference: Chapter 7

---

### **B**

**BaRP (Bandit Routing Protocol)**
- Economic routing algorithm in Router
- Equivalent to PLIx Economic Router Gate
- Reference: Chapter 5, Chapter 11

**Bitemporal**
- Dual time tracking (transaction time + valid time)
- Used in CMC and SEG for time-travel queries
- Reference: Chapter 9, Chapter 12

---

### **C**

**CMC (Context Memory Core)**
- AIM-OS bitemporal memory system
- Transforms with PLIx: From fact storage to intent memory
- Reference: Chapter 9

**CNL (Controlled Natural Language)**
- Human-readable language for expressing PLIx contracts
- Gherkin-style grammar
- Reference: Chapter 6, Chapter 13

**Consciousness (AI)**
- Self-awareness: Knowing what you want
- Intent awareness: Understanding your motivations
- Self-verification: Verifying your success
- Reference: Chapter 17, Chapter 23

**Contract (PLIx)**
- Formal specification of intent
- Includes preconditions, postconditions, constraints
- Reference: Chapter 5, Chapter 8

---

### **D**

**Design by Contract (DbC)**
- Programming paradigm using pre/post conditions
- Foundation of PLIx Contract Layer
- Reference: Chapter 5

**Durable Execution**
- Execution that survives failures
- Checkpointing and recovery
- Reference: Chapter 14

---

### **E**

**Evidence Chain**
- Graph of evidence supporting claims
- Stored in SEG
- Reference: Chapter 12, Chapter 15

**Execution Plan**
- Sequence of steps to achieve intent
- Generated from PLIx contract
- Reference: Chapter 8, Chapter 11

---

### **F**

**Formal Validation**
- Mathematical proof of correctness
- Using Alloy, TLA+, SMT solvers
- Reference: Chapter 7

**Four Pillars**
- Contract Layer: Design by Contract, CNL/DSLs
- Execution Layer: Durable Execution, Saga Pattern
- Safety Layer: LLM Confidence, Policy-as-Code
- Evidence Layer: Provenance, Evidence Chains
- Reference: Chapter 5

---

### **G**

**Gherkin**
- Behavior-driven development language
- Inspiration for CNL grammar
- Reference: Chapter 6

---

### **H**

**HHNI (Hierarchical Hypergraph Neural Index)**
- AIM-OS semantic indexing system
- Used for tool routing and context retrieval
- Reference: Chapter 9

---

### **I**

**Intent**
- What we want to achieve
- Expressed in PLIx contracts
- Separate from execution (how we achieve it)
- Reference: Chapter 2, Chapter 4

**Intent Achievement**
- Successfully achieving an intent
- Verified through postcondition checking
- Reference: Chapter 10, Chapter 11

**Intent Awareness**
- Knowing what you want
- Foundation of AI consciousness
- Reference: Chapter 17, Chapter 23

**Intent Confidence**
- Confidence in intent achievement
- Tracked by VIF
- Reference: Chapter 10

**Intent Evolution**
- How intents change over time
- Tracked in TCS and SEG
- Reference: Chapter 20

**Intent Lineage**
- Tracing outcomes back to intents
- Stored in SEG
- Reference: Chapter 12, Chapter 15

**Intent Verification**
- Verifying that intent was achieved
- Through postcondition checking
- Reference: Chapter 10, Chapter 19

**IR (Intermediate Representation)**
- PLIx internal representation
- Between CNL and execution plans
- Reference: Chapter 8

## 📊 Quick Reference

### **Essential Concepts**

1. **Pure Language:** Expresses intent without implementation contamination
2. **Intent:** What we want to achieve (separate from execution)
3. **PLIx:** Pure language for expressing intent
4. **Consciousness:** Self-awareness through intent awareness
5. **Trust:** Confidence based on verifiable intent achievement

### **Key Systems**

1. **CMC:** Intent-aware memory storage
2. **VIF:** Intent-aware verification
3. **APOE:** Intent-aware orchestration
4. **SEG:** Intent-aware evidence tracking

### **Key Processes**

1. **CNL → Contract:** Human-readable to formal contract
2. **Contract → Plan:** Intent to execution plan
3. **Plan → Execution:** Execution with verification
4. **Execution → Evidence:** Evidence collection and lineage
