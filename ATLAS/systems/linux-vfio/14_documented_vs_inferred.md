# Documented vs inferred

## DOCUMENTED

**Kernel** **VFIO** **documentation** **in** **the** **mainline** **docs** **tree** (`src-linux-vfio-kernel-doc`).

## INFERRED

**Exact** **QEMU** **or** **Firecracker** **CLI** **for** **a** **specific** **NIC** **or** **GPU** **passthrough** **layout** **without** **reading** **that** **invocation.**
