# Documented vs inferred

## DOCUMENTED

**Kernel** **vhost** **driver** **API** **documentation** **in** **the** **mainline** **tree** (`src-linux-vhost-kernel-doc`).

## INFERRED

**Exact** **vhost-user** **socket** **layout** **and** **memory** **region** **setup** **for** **a** **specific** **QEMU** **command** **line** **without** **reading** **that** **invocation.**
