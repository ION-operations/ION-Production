## Comparative Reading

1. The stack separates cleanly by altitude: master program for campaign law, packets for directive shifts, work packages for bounded execution, findings boards for condensed local reading, and detailed reports for proof.
2. The strongest course-change family is packet surfaces, not the master program and not the findings boards.
3. The strongest synthesis family is findings boards, while the strongest evidence family is detailed reports.
4. The main redundancy risk is not pure duplication of role; it is the accumulation of many serial packets, boards, and reports that must be read together to restore full context.
