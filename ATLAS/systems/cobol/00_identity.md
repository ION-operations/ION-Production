# COBOL — Identity

**Kind:** **Imperative, procedural** programming language family aimed at **business**, **finance**, and **administrative** computing; **English-like** syntax; **long-running** mainframe deployment base (`DOCUMENTED`, `src-wiki-cobol`).

**Standards:** **COBOL** standards through **ISO** / **ANSI** / **INCITS** history — **COBOL 2014** etc. (`DOCUMENTED`, `src-wiki-cobol`).

## Boundaries

- **Not** **SQL** — different language (interoperation common).  
- **Not** PL/I — distinct design; **PL/I** competed with COBOL **record/report** goals (`HISTORICAL`, `systems/pl-i`).

## Why this system matters

- **Dominant** **batch** and **transaction** language for **legacy** enterprise systems — **migration** and **interop** remain operational concerns (`DOCUMENTED`, `src-wiki-cobol`).  
- **Influenced** **PL/I** unification efforts on IBM **System/360** (`HISTORICAL`).

## What this system teaches the atlas

- **Longevity** of **data format** and **decimal arithmetic** requirements in **regulated** domains (`DOCUMENTED` pattern).
