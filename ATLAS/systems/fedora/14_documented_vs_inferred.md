# Documented vs inferred

## DOCUMENTED

Fedora **releases** **documentation** **and** **systemd** **quick** **docs** (`src-fedora-releases`, `src-fedora-systemd-quick-docs`); **DNF** **user** **documentation** (`src-dnf-user-guide`).

## INFERRED

**Minimal** **container** **images** **or** **spins** **that** **change** **init** **or** **libc** **defaults** **without** **reading** **that** **maintainer’s** **documentation**.
