## 📊 WHY THE CHANGE?

### Historical System (GOAL 1-5) - 2025-10-22 to 2025-10-28

**Characteristics:**
- **Task-oriented:** "Tag VIF", "Tag CMC", specific actions
- **Sequential:** GOAL 1 → GOAL 2 → GOAL 3 → GOAL 4 → GOAL 5
- **Short-term:** Focused on immediate tagging work
- **Narrow scope:** Individual system tagging
- **Fast iteration:** Complete goals in hours/days

**Benefits:**
- Quick wins (GOAL 1-4 completed in ~7 hours total!)
- Clear next steps
- Easy tracking
- Momentum building

**Limitations:**
- Doesn't capture strategic objectives (CMC completion is more than tagging)
- Hard to prioritize (all goals seem equal)
- Doesn't show system relationships
- Limited to tagging work

---

### Current System (OBJ-01-14) - 2025-10-29 to Present

**Characteristics:**
- **Strategic objectives:** Complete systems, not just tasks
- **Parallel execution:** Can work on multiple OBJs simultaneously
- **Long-term:** Target dates weeks/months out
- **Broad scope:** Complete system implementations
- **Priority hierarchy:** TIER S/A/B/C for clear focus
- **Rich metadata:** KRs, artifacts, evidence, dependencies

**Benefits:**
- Strategic alignment (all OBJs trace to north star)
- Clear priorities (TIER S = ship-critical)
- Parallel work (can advance multiple OBJs)
- Better tracking (completion %, KPIs)
- System-level thinking (complete implementations)

**Limitations:**
- More complex (requires more metadata)
- Slower iteration (objectives take weeks, not hours)

**Why This Is Better:**
- Scales to 70+ systems (GOAL 1-5 wouldn't scale)
- Enables prioritization (TIER S/A/B/C critical for ship focus)
- Shows relationships (dependencies, enables, invariants)
- Professional project management (KPIs, tracking, dashboards)
