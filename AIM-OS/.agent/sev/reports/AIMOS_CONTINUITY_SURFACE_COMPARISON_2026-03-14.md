## Direct Comparative Reading

### Chat docs vs capsules

- Chat docs are richer and denser than capsules for reconstructing what happened, what files were produced, and which IDs were generated.
- Capsules are sharper than chat for bounded recovery because `MISSION`, `NOW`, `MUST-NOT`, `EVIDENCE`, `NEXT`, and `HANDOFF` are constrained fields instead of narrative prose.
- Together they form the strongest file-based rolling continuity pair in the visible organism.

### Status files vs `current_priorities.md`

- Status files are better for quick "what is this lane doing right now?" checks when they are fresh.
- `current_priorities.md` is better for preserving standing intent, waiting conditions, and ongoing strategic focus across turns.
- Both families are visibly vulnerable to lag: local examples still carry pre-consolidation or early-consolidation framing while later chat and capsules have advanced much further.

### File-based surfaces vs MCP-backed continuity

- File-based continuity is durable, inspectable, and visible even without the live bus.
- MCP-backed continuity is strongest for live assignment, cross-agent visibility, and machine-readable retrieval while transport is healthy.
- The current local evidence does not show one flat MCP win, because messages are fresh while memory recall and timeline recency are weaker than expected for today's lane.

## Net Comparative Answer

1. Chat docs anchor dense audit-trail continuity.
2. Capsules anchor bounded handoff and anti-drift continuity.
3. Status files anchor quick snapshot continuity when they are fresh.
4. `current_priorities.md` anchors standing-order continuity more than immediate session truth.
5. MCP-backed continuity anchors live cross-agent and machine-readable continuity, but its message, memory, and timeline sub-surfaces are not equally strong right now.

These are comparative role answers only. They do not select one final continuity source of truth.
