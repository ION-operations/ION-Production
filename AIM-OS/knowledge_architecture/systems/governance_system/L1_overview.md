# L1 Overview: Governance System

## Purpose
The Governance System provides comprehensive system governance and oversight capabilities for AIM-OS, including policy management, compliance checking, audit systems, and regulatory oversight to ensure proper system operation and adherence to governance principles.

## Core Functionality

### Policy Management
- **Policy Creation:** Create and define governance policies across the system
- **Policy Updates:** Update and modify existing policies
- **Policy Enforcement:** Automated enforcement of governance policies
- **Policy Lifecycle:** Manage complete policy lifecycle from creation to retirement

### Compliance Checking
- **Automated Compliance:** Automated compliance verification across all systems
- **Compliance Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring of compliance status
- **Violation Detection:** Identification and reporting of compliance violations
- **Compliance Reporting:** Comprehensive reporting on compliance status

### Audit Systems
- **Comprehensive Auditing:** Complete auditing and logging of system operations
- **Audit Trail:** Complete audit trail for all governance activities
- **Audit Reporting:** Detailed audit reports and analysis
- **Audit Integrity:** Maintain audit trail integrity and security

### Regulatory Oversight
- **Regulatory Monitoring:** Monitoring and enforcement of regulatory requirements
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Ensure compliance with all applicable regulations
- **Regulatory Reporting:** Generate regulatory compliance reports
- **Regulatory Updates:** Stay current with regulatory changes

### Governance Decision Making
- **Decision Engine:** Make governance decisions based on policies and context
- **Decision Rationale:** Document decision rationale and reasoning
- **Decision Consistency:** Ensure consistent decision-making across the system
- **Decision Audit:** Maintain complete audit trail of all decisions

### Approval Workflow Management
- **Workflow Management:** Manage approval workflows for governance decisions
- **Authorization Control:** Ensure proper authorization and oversight
- **Workflow Efficiency:** Optimize approval workflow processes
- **Workflow Audit:** Maintain complete audit trail of approval processes

## Integration Points

### With SDF-CVF
- **Quality Gates:** Uses SDF-CVF for quality gates and quartet parity
- **Governance Validation:** Validates governance through SDF-CVF quality gates
- **Documentation Compliance:** Ensures documentation compliance through quartet parity

### With SCOR
- **Safety Validation:** Integrates with SCOR for safety validation
- **Policy Enforcement:** Uses SCOR for policy enforcement and safety gates
- **Compliance Monitoring:** Leverages SCOR for compliance monitoring

### With CMC
- **Governance Storage:** Stores governance data in CMC
- **Audit Trail Storage:** Maintains audit trails in CMC
- **Policy Storage:** Stores policy definitions in CMC

### With Confidence-Gated Controls
- **Policy Integration:** Provides policies for confidence-gated controls
- **Governance Validation:** Validates changes through governance policies
- **Approval Integration:** Integrates with approval workflows
