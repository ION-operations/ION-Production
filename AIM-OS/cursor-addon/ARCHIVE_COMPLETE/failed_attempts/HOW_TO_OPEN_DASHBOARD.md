# 🎯 HOW TO OPEN THE DASHBOARD - SUPER SIMPLE GUIDE

**Last Updated:** 2025-11-01

---

## 📍 **WHERE THE DASHBOARD IS**

The dashboard appears in the **RIGHT SIDEBAR** when you click the **✨ sparkle icon** in the Activity Bar (left side).

---

## 🚀 **HOW TO OPEN IT (3 WAYS)**

### **Method 1: Click the Sparkle Icon** ⭐ (EASIEST)

1. Look at the **LEFT side** of Cursor (Activity Bar)
2. Find the **✨ sparkle icon** (it says "AIM-OS" when you hover)
3. **Click it**
4. The **RIGHT SIDEBAR** will open
5. You should see **"Dashboard"** in the sidebar
6. **Click "Dashboard"**

**That's it!** The dashboard should appear.

---

### **Method 2: Command Palette** ⌨️

1. Press **`Ctrl+Shift+P`** (Windows) or **`Cmd+Shift+P`** (Mac)
2. Type: **`AIM-OS: Show Dashboard`**
3. Press **Enter**

**That's it!** The dashboard should appear.

---

### **Method 3: Right-Click Activity Bar** 🖱️

1. Right-click on the **✨ sparkle icon** in Activity Bar
2. Select **"Dashboard"**

---

## 🔍 **WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE**

When it opens correctly, you should see:

- **React UI with tabs:** Agents, Chat, Chains, Tools, Timeline, NL Tags
- **NOT blank** - Full dashboard interface

---

## ❌ **IF IT'S BLANK**

If the right panel opens but shows **blank/empty**:

1. Check the **Output panel** (bottom):
   - Click **"Output"** tab at bottom
   - Select **"AIM-OS Dashboard"** from dropdown
   - Look for `[AIM-OS] ✅ resolveWebviewView CALLED` message

2. If you DON'T see that message:
   - The webview isn't resolving
   - Extension might not be activated
   - Try reloading: **Ctrl+Shift+P → Developer: Reload Window**
