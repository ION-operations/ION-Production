## 🔬 **CREATION PROCESS**

### **Complete Handoff Process (20-40 minutes)**

**Step 1: Work Summary (5-10 min)**
- What was accomplished this session?
- What decisions were made?
- What was learned?
- What documentation created?

**Step 2: Mental Model Capture (10-15 min)**
- For each active system: What do I understand?
- What's in progress?
- What's unclear?
- What are next steps?

**Step 3: State Capture (5-10 min)**
- Emotional state honestly
- Cognitive load assessment
- Confidence levels
- Energy and fatigue

**Step 4: Priority Setting (3-5 min)**
- What should next session do first?
- Why in that order?
- Any critical context?
- Any urgent items?

**Step 5: Personal Message (2-5 min)**
- Write to future self
- Share insights
- Express emotions
- Maintain identity

**Step 6: Validate (2-3 min)**
- Could I restore from this?
- Is context complete?
- Are priorities clear?
- Is emotional continuity preserved?
