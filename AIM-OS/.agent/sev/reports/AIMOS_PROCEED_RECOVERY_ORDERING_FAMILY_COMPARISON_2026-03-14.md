## Direct Comparative Reading

- Fastest locally bounded proceed recovery orderings are strongest when the question is "what has to come back first to restore fast bounded proceed as quickly as possible?"
- Safest human-readable bounded proceed recovery orderings are strongest when the question is "what has to come back first to make bounded proceed read as safe to a human again?"
- Clearest justified handoff proceed recovery orderings are strongest when the question is "what has to come back first to make the handoff read as justified again?"
- Strongest near-live synthesized proceed recovery orderings are strongest when the question is "what has to come back first to restore live-plus-synthesis proceed again?"
- Fullest visible corroborated proceed sufficiency recovery orderings are strongest when the question is "what has to come back first versus later to restore the fullest visible proceed reading again?"

## Visible False-Release, Overclaim, Ordering Strictness, Heaviness, And Narrowing Risks

1. Fastest locally bounded proceed recovery orderings restore the narrowest viable proceed reading first, but later justification can still be absent.
2. Safest human-readable bounded proceed recovery orderings restore readable trust quickly, but prose can make the sequence look more complete than it is.
3. Clearest justified handoff proceed recovery orderings restore rationale first, but can look defensible before enough live freshness returns.
4. Strongest near-live synthesized proceed recovery orderings restore live-plus-synthesis first, but can remain too thin in bounded handoff detail.
5. Fullest visible corroborated proceed sufficiency recovery orderings restore the broadest visible sequence, but they also carry the highest coordination cost and the strongest temptation to be mistaken for policy rather than evidence.

- Safest human-readable bounded proceed recovery orderings were treated as human-usable bounded orderings, not as the freshest or broadest orderings.

- Strongest near-live synthesized proceed recovery orderings were treated as hybrid live-plus-synthesis orderings, not as the fullest corroborated orderings.
- Fullest visible corroborated proceed sufficiency recovery orderings were treated as the broadest visible orderings, not as a canon winner.
