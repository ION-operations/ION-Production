\section{Scoring Function}

The refined retrieval stage uses a weighted combination of factors:

\textbf{Base Formula:}
\begin{equation}
\text{score} = w_c \cdot \text{content} + w_a \cdot \text{authority} + w_t \cdot \text{temporal} + w_s \cdot \text{structure}
\end{equation}

Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $w_c$ = content weight (default: 0.4)
    \item $w_a$ = authority weight (default: 0.3)
    \item $w_t$ = temporal weight (default: 0.2)
    \item $w_s$ = structure weight (default: 0.1)
\end{itemize}

\section{Authority Scoring Model}

The authority scoring model combines multiple factors:

\begin{equation}
\text{authority}(a, c) = w_e \cdot \text{evidence} + w_v \cdot \text{validation} + w_p \cdot \text{peer} + w_c \cdot \text{context\_fit}
\end{equation}

With default weights:
\begin{align}
w_e &= 0.4 \quad \text{(evidence weight)} \\
w_v &= 0.3 \quad \text{(validation weight)} \\
w_p &= 0.2 \quad \text{(peer weight)} \\
w_c &= 0.1 \quad \text{(context fit weight)}
\end{align}

\section{Learning Rate Differential Equation}

Self-improvement dynamics follow a differential equation:

\begin{equation}
\frac{dQ}{dt} = \alpha \cdot (\text{benefit} - \text{cost}) - \beta \cdot \text{regression}
\end{equation}

Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $Q$ = quality metric
    \item $\alpha$ = learning rate coefficient
    \item $\beta$ = regression penalty coefficient
    \item $\text{benefit}$ = improvement benefit
    \item $\text{cost}$ = improvement cost
    \item $\text{regression}$ = quality regression rate
\end{itemize}

\section{Graph Formal Definition}

The semantic evidence graph is formally defined as:

\begin{equation}
G = (V, E, \tau_{TT}, \tau_{VT}, \theta, \sigma, \varepsilon)
\end{equation}

Where:
\begin{align}
V &= C \cup S \cup D \cup A \quad \text{(Claims, Sources, Derivations, Agents)} \\
E &\subseteq V \times V \times \text{EdgeTypes} \quad \text{(Directed edges with types)} \\
\tau_{TT} &: V \cup E \to \text{Timestamps} \quad \text{(Transaction time)} \\
\tau_{VT} &: V \to \text{Intervals} \quad \text{(Valid time)} \\
\theta &: V \to \text{Content} \quad \text{(Node content function)} \\
\sigma &: E \to [0, 1] \quad \text{(Edge strength function)} \\
\varepsilon &: C \to \mathbb{R}^d \quad \text{(Claim embedding function)}
\end{align}

\section{Bayesian Update}

Confidence calibration uses Bayesian updates:

\begin{equation}
\text{posterior} = \frac{\text{prior} \cdot \text{likelihood}}{\text{evidence}}
\end{equation}

With prior distribution:
\begin{equation}
\text{prior} = \text{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)
\end{equation}

Where $\alpha$ = successes, $\beta$ = failures, and:
\begin{equation}
E[\text{prior}] = \frac{\alpha}{\alpha + \beta}
\end{equation}
