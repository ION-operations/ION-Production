It enforces policies consistently, makes governance decisions based on rules and context, manages approval workflows, and monitors compliance across all systems. The system integrates with SDF-CVF for quality gates, SCOR for safety validation, CMC for persistent storage, and Confidence-Gated Controls for policy enforcement.
