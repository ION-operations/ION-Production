# Scope

## In scope

- Architecture and protection concepts documented in cited primary literature.  
- Historical deployment context at participating sites (only where sourced).  
- Comparative hooks to later kernels **as labeled HISTORICAL or INFERRED**.

## Out of scope

- Hardware maintenance, site-specific anecdotes without citations.  
- Any claim of equivalence to modern POSIX behavior unless sourced.

## Versioning note

Multics underwent continuous evolution across installations; treat “the design” as **family** not a single frozen release (`HISTORICAL`).
