# AIMOS Status-Classification Surface Best-At Map - 2026-03-14

Work package: `CONSOLIDATION_WORK_PACKAGE_24_2026-03-14`

This map answers what each status-classification family appears best at locally.
It does not select a final status canon.

| Surface family | Best at locally | Narrower than siblings where | Unique status-classification value preserved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Broad verdict-map surfaces | Best at naming whole-system state in compact, high-level verdict language | They are weaker than live fields at literal precision and weaker than verification cards at tightly bounded class meaning | They preserve the broadest human verdict vocabulary across the organism |

## Best-At Answer

- Broad verdict maps are best at broad verdict language.
- Bounded verification-result surfaces are best at bounded result language.
- Degraded registers are best at weak-state language.
- Live machine status fields are best at current field-state language.
- Package-test or assertion-outcome surfaces are best at assertion-outcome language.

## Local Constraint

No single status-classification family stands alone as total language truth on this host:

- broad verdict maps drift fastest,
- bounded cards are disciplined but dated,
- degraded registers are expressive but selective,
- live fields are exact but semantically thin,
- and package assertions are crisp but local.
