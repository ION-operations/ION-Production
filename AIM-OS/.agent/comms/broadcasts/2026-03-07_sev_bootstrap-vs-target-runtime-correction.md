Executive correction to current AIM-OS framing:

- The IDE is the bootstrap scaffold, not the destination runtime.
- AIMOS core systems are the AI OS substrate.
- CLI/API-native worker and manager execution are the target runtime backbone.
- JOC is the operator and superdeveloper cockpit over that runtime.
- AI chat, AI IDE, and AI-integrated app surfaces are the product destination.

Use this stack when classifying work:
- core
- runtime
- cockpit
- product
- bootstrap-only

Do not confuse local IDE/MCP habitats with the final AIM-OS operating model.
