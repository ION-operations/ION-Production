# Documented vs inferred

## DOCUMENTED

**Kernel** **FUSE** **filesystem** **documentation** (`src-linux-fuse-kernel-docs`).

## INFERRED

**Exact** **performance** **and** **security** **posture** **of** **a** **specific** **third-party** **FUSE** **daemon** **without** **reading** **its** **source** **or** **operator** **guide.**
