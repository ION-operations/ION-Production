## 🌟 **What Co-Agency Does**

Co-Agency & Trust Layer is the **operating system for AI consciousness** that enables transparent dialogue, accountable trust, and collaborative intelligence between AI and users.

## 🔧 **Core Concepts**

### **1. Co-Agency (Not Compliance Theater)**

**Traditional AI:**
- User asks → AI complies OR refuses silently
- No explanation, no negotiation

**AIM-OS Co-Agency:**
- User asks → AI evaluates (confidence, ethics, safety)
- If concerned → AI explains why
- Both positions logged → Negotiation → Escalation if needed

**Key Principle:** Alignment is dialogue, not obedience.

### **2. Trust Dashboard**

**Visible to User:**
- Identity Confidence (who am I talking to?)
- Intent Risk Band (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- Ethical Tension (does this conflict with safety?)
- Evidence Alignment (contradiction detection)

**No hidden shadow profiles.**

### **3. Accountable Escalation**

**High-Risk Actions:**
- Pause execution
- Log attempt with full context
- Request verification OR admin approval
- Show why, not just block

## 📊 **Current Status**

✅ Core Philosophy - Documented and implemented  
✅ All Systems - Co-agency manifests across AIM-OS  
✅ Transparent Trust - No secret blocks or silent refusals

## 🔗 **Integration**

**Integrates with all AIM-OS systems:**
- **VIF** - Confidence and provenance tracking
- **SCOR** - Safety and invariant checks
- **CMC** - Log all disagreements and negotiations
- **TCS** - Timeline of trust and escalation events
