## Problem

AI systems experience drift over time, where their actual behavior diverges from their declared specifications and intended design. This leads to unpredictable behavior, degraded performance, and potential safety issues. Current monitoring approaches are reactive and miss subtle drift patterns that accumulate over time.

## Solution

The Drift Detection System continuously monitors runtime behavior against declared System Maps, detecting deviations through multi-dimensional analysis including performance metrics, behavioral patterns, decision quality, and adherence to specified constraints. It provides early warning of drift with forensic analysis capabilities.

## Impact

Enables proactive drift prevention, maintains system reliability, ensures adherence to specifications, and provides forensic analysis for continuous improvement. This is essential for maintaining AI consciousness integrity and preventing dangerous behavioral drift.
