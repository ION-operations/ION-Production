## 🌟 **THE VISION**

Build **the most impressive document editor** that combines:

1. **Full LaTeX Math Rendering** (like Overleaf) - Complex equations, formulas, mathematical notation
2. **Rich Text Styles** - All text formatting capabilities (bold, italic, headings, lists, tables, etc.)
3. **AI-Powered Management** - AI can navigate, edit, organize, and manage documents intelligently
4. **Advanced Tagging & Organization** - Multi-dimensional tagging, hierarchical organization, semantic search
5. **Section-Based Editing** - Edit documents in sections, isolate changes, track modifications
6. **Visual Diff/Edit Tracking** (like Monaco) - See edits, changes, history with visual indicators

**But we're going BEYOND this vision to create something truly revolutionary.**

#### **3. AI-Powered Document Intelligence**
- **Semantic understanding** (HHNI integration for document structure)

- **Multi-dimensional tags** (topic, type, status, priority, author, date, etc.)

- **Granular editing** (edit individual sections independently)

## 🚨 **RISKS & MITIGATION**

### **Risk 1: Performance**
- **Risk:** Large documents may be slow
- **Mitigation:** Virtual scrolling, lazy loading, section-based rendering

### **Risk 2: Math Rendering**
- **Risk:** Complex equations may not render correctly
- **Mitigation:** KaTeX + MathJax hybrid, fallback rendering

### **Risk 3: Collaboration Conflicts**
- **Risk:** Concurrent edits may conflict
- **Mitigation:** CRDT (Yjs), section locking, visual merge

### **Risk 4: AI Accuracy**
- **Risk:** AI suggestions may be incorrect
- **Mitigation:** Confidence thresholds, user approval, VIF witnesses
