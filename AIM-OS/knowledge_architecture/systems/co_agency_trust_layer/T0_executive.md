# Co-Agency & Trust Layer – T0 Executive Summary (≈100 words)

Co-Agency & Trust Layer enables transparent disagreement, accountable escalation, and trust relationship management for AI-human collaboration. Provides trust dashboard, disagreement signaling, and escalation protocols. Enables AI to explain concerns transparently, maintain accountable relationships, and escalate appropriately. Key features include transparent disagreement, trust relationship monitoring, accountable escalation paths, evidence-based risk assessment, and human-readable explanations. Agent Identity Protocol ensures proper context attribution and continuity across trust interactions.
