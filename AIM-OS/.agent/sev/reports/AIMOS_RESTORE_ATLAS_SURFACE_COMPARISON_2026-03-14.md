## Comparative Reading

1. The five families separate cleanly by restore role: startup gates for entry, lane-local indexes for one workspace, synthesized restore for current reading, atlas/registers for map truth, and live MCP for immediate state.
2. The strongest startup family is the startup-gate layer; the strongest campaign-restore family is the synthesized restore layer.
3. The strongest lane-local restore family is the SEV README/INDEX layer, even though it shows the sharpest local filesystem drift.
4. The strongest structural-truth family is atlas/register surfaces, while the strongest freshness family is live MCP restore, driven mostly by messages rather than memory or timeline.
5. The restore problem is compositional rather than solved by one family: no single sampled surface combines startup order, lane context, broad map truth, and fresh live state without depending on siblings.
