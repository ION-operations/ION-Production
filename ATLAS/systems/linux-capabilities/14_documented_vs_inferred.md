# Documented vs inferred

## DOCUMENTED

**capabilities(7)** **manual** **page** **and** **kernel** **credential** **documentation** **cross-references** (`src-linux-capabilities-man7`).

## INFERRED

**Exact** **default** **capability** **sets** **for** **a** **given** **container** **runtime** **version** **without** **reading** **its** **release** **notes.**
