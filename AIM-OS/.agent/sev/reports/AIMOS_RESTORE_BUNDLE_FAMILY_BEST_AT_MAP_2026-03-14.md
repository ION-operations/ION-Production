## Local Answer

No single restore-bundle family is universally sufficient locally.
The visible stack separates cleanly by restore role:

- startup plus live bus is the safest cold-start restore
- live bus plus active work package is the fastest exact active-task restore
- capsule plus active work package is the safest bounded handoff restore
- chat plus cited artifacts is the richest readable packet restore
- findings board plus proof report is the strongest synthesized evidence-backed restore

The local restore answer is therefore bundle-compositional, not singular.
