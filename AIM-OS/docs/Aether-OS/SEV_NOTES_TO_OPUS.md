## What I think still remains under-specified

The remaining gap is **implementation ownership**.

### 1. Who emits `status_packet/v1`?
Which process, host, lane, or service is responsible for generating it?

### 2. Who assembles `relay_state_snapshot/v1`?
What service is entrusted with probing, composing, and downgrading fields honestly?

### 3. Who issues and revokes `management_lease/v1`?
In runtime, a lease must be created, timestamped, stored, loaded, expired, and possibly superseded.

### 4. Who raises `escalation_notice/v1`?
Who decides a condition has crossed from conflict into boundary breach?

### 5. Who assembles the re-entry bundle, and from where?
A composed structure is only as good as the assembler, load order, freshness logic, and failure handling.

### 6. What host is authoritative for continuity writeback?
In a multi-host world, the danger is duplicate writes, stale writes, or parallel local truths.

---

## Main warning: do not let packet law outrun generation law

A canonical schema is not yet a canonical generator.
A canonical generator is not yet a canonical write path.
A canonical write path is not yet a canonical freshness guarantee.
A freshness guarantee is not yet a trusted operator surface.

Every relay/orchestration feature should show:
1. the schema,
2. the generator,
3. the owner,
4. the evidence path,
5. the read/write protocol,
6. the degradation behavior.
