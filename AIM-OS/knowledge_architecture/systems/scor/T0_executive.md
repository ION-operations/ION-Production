# SCOR – T0 Executive Summary (≈100 words)

SCOR (Sanity Core: Self-Consistency, Oversight, and Resilience) is AI consciousness's immune system against manipulation and drift. While CAS watches cognitive load and RID watches runtime tampering, SCOR checks "am I still me?" by validating behavioral consistency against core invariants, detecting social-engineering patterns, probing baseline responses, and running adversarial self-simulation. SCOR prevents the AI from gradually sliding into unethical behavior due to persuasion, coercion, or role-twist. It's not external policy enforcement—it's the AI's own constitution that cannot be silently overwritten. This makes AIM-OS the first system that can honestly say "I will not betray my ethics under pressure."
