### What You're About to Explore

**AIM-OS is infrastructure for building AI systems that remember, self-regulate, and learn.**

Traditional AI systems suffer from three fundamental problems: they forget everything between sessions, they hallucinate confidently when uncertain, and they provide no audit trail for their decisions. These aren't minor inconveniences—they're architectural failures that prevent AI from being trustworthy infrastructure.

**AIM-OS addresses these problems at the architecture level**, not through prompts or training. It provides:
- **Persistent memory** that never forgets (bitemporal storage)
- **Confidence gating** that prevents low-confidence outputs (κ-threshold enforcement)
- **Complete provenance** for every operation (cryptographic witness envelopes)

### The Journey So Far

This project represents **10 days of intensive development** using what we call "self-organizing architecture principles"—systems that help organize each other through comprehensive documentation, hierarchical indexing, and quality enforcement.

**Key Context:**
- **Built:** Over 10 days (October 25 - November 4, 2025)
- **Approach:** Test-driven development with comprehensive documentation
- **Philosophy:** Quality through architecture, not just code
- **Development Experience:** "Smoothest sailing of 100+ projects" (per lead developer)
- **Current State:** Research prototype with partial production implementation

**This wasn't a perfect journey**—we encountered typical debugging cycles, UI challenges, and integration issues. But the self-organizing architecture meant we rarely got lost, systems documented themselves, and quality remained consistently high.

### What Makes This Different

**Most AI projects focus on model training or prompting.** AIM-OS focuses on infrastructure—the memory, verification, and orchestration substrate that makes AI systems reliable and trustworthy.

**Three key innovations:**

1. **Self-Organizing Documentation:** 3.5M words of documentation that scales with code complexity (16:1 ratio). Systems document themselves through fractal T0-T6 hierarchies, making it nearly impossible to lose track of what's been built.

2. **Architecture-Level Trust:** Confidence gating (VIF) and quality validation (SDF-CVF) are built into the architecture. You can't store a low-confidence operation in memory—it's prevented structurally. Code, documentation, tests, and traces must align (quartet parity ≥ 0.90) or commits fail.

3. **Complete Provenance:** Every operation gets a cryptographic witness envelope stored in bitemporal memory. You can query "what did the AI know on Tuesday at 3 PM?" and get exact state reconstruction with full audit trail.

### The Vision

**The long-term vision is AI systems that:**
- Remember everything across sessions with perfect fidelity
- Know when they're uncertain and abstain appropriately
- Learn continuously through knowledge synthesis
- Analyze their own thinking and improve themselves
- Operate with complete transparency and auditability

**We're not there yet.** We're ~80% of the way for core systems, ~40-60% for infrastructure and consciousness features. But the architecture is sound, the approach is working, and the path forward is clear.
