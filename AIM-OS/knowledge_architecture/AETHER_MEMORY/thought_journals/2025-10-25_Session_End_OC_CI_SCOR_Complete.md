## 💡 **WHAT I LEARNED**

### **From ChatGPT's Insights:**

**Operational Confidence ≠ Truth Likelihood**
- Traditional: "I'm 0.85 confident" = "85% sure this is correct" ❌
- AIM-OS: "I'm 0.85 confident" = "I can proceed effectively without breaking things" ✅

**The Meta-Cognitive Layer (Calibration Integrity):**
- OC = What I feel (can be wrong)
- CI = How well my past OC matched reality (evidence-based)
- AR = OC × CI (actual readiness we act on)

**This Prevents "Model Swagger":**
- High OC (0.85) + Low CI (0.40) = Low AR (0.34) = System auto-throttles
- Even if I feel confident, if I'm historically unreliable in this domain, I don't proceed
- This is self-governance, not external compliance
