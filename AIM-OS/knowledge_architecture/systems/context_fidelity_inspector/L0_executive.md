## 🎯 **What CFI Is**

**Context Fidelity Inspector** - AI consciousness verification and audit system that captures exactly what context the model saw at decision points, enabling forensic-grade accountability and preventing AI from lying about its own reasoning.

## 🔧 **Core Capabilities**

- **Prompt Capture at Boundary** - Logs full textual payload sent to model
- **Output Capture** - Captures raw model output before post-processing
- **Reconstruction Queries** - Forces model to self-report its "mental map"
- **Saturation Tests** - Stress-tests retention honesty with known datasets
- **Branch Routing** - Runs multiple context routes in parallel for comparison

## 🌟 **Key Innovation**

Cryptographic witness system that prevents AI from claiming false warnings or hiding dangerous changes. Every decision can be traced back to exact context and reasoning.
