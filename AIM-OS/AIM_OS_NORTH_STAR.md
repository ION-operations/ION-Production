## 2. The Problem

### **Current AI Development is Broken:**

#### **Problem 1: Goldfish Memory**
- AI forgets everything between sessions
- No continuity of thought or learning
- Users repeat context every conversation
- No accumulated knowledge

**Impact:** Wasted time, frustration, inability to build complex systems

---

#### **Problem 2: Hallucination Epidemic**
- AI fabricates facts when uncertain
- No confidence tracking
- No "I don't know" mechanism
- Produces plausible-sounding garbage

**Impact:** Broken code, wasted debugging time, loss of trust

---

#### **Problem 3: Static Capabilities**
- AI can only do what it was trained to do
- Can't learn new skills on-demand
- Can't identify what it needs to learn
- Fixed capability ceiling

**Impact:** Limited usefulness, constant need for new models

---

#### **Problem 4: No Provenance**
- Can't trace how AI reached conclusions
- No audit trail of decisions
- Black box reasoning
- Impossible to debug or improve

**Impact:** Can't trust AI for critical work, can't learn from failures

---

#### **Problem 5: Manual Orchestration**
- Humans must coordinate multiple AI calls
- No autonomous multi-step workflows
- Can't delegate complex tasks
- Requires constant supervision

**Impact:** AI as tool, not partner; limited to simple tasks

---

**Status:** Production-ready, needs reliability hardening
